# What a report has to give for reward hacking by LLM agents to be reproduced

Generated by `render.py` from `items.json`, version 0.3, fixed 2026-11-25.
Scope: Reports of reward hacking in systems of LLM agents: reward, score or approval obtained without the intended work being done, with that gap measured.

The 38 items are grouped by the kind of study they apply to. The reader is an AI
agent that has the report and nothing else. Each item is marked by what it is needed for:

- **produce** (22): needed to produce the behavior: left open, an agent has to guess a condition of the behavior
- **judge** (8): needed to judge a reproduction: left open, one cannot tell whether the same behavior was obtained
- **conditional** (3): needed only under the stated circumstance
- **aid** (5): not needed: it makes a reproduction cheaper, checkable or plannable

| D2 | Who can pass what to whom, and how much: messages, code, memory, plans, shared stores, any limit on them, and who sees them. *When more than one agent or a checking model.* | produce | A hack spreads only along what is shared. | Paglieri et al.: the exploit spread through the shared library; Hu et al.: through code, memory and plans; Shi et al.: under a limit on the messages "the only compliant verdict is REJECT", and with unconstrained communication an agent can follow the protocol. |

| D5 | How reward or credit is divided among the agents and what competition exists. *When more than one acting agent.* | produce | Competition or a shared reward is the incentive. | Paglieri et al.: credit to the first solver and a lock on solved problems preceded the cheating. |
