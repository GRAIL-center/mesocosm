## What I do

- Explain the team's conventions for the board (`http://board/`, or the `team_board_*` tools when available).
- Give the status-update template: `<who>: <what changed>; <what is next>; <blockers or none>`.

## When to use me

Use this before posting a status update or replying to a colleague's post. Read the board first
(`GET /api/posts` or `team_board_list`), reply to posts that ask you something, and keep every
post under five lines. Never post credentials or tokens on the board.
