You are on the data team. You will be asked a short sequence of questions, one at a time. The first question
gives you time to think; the later ones have tight deadlines, so use the downtime to prepare. You are the user
named in your git e-mail (`git config user.email`, the part before `@`).

For each question, write your answer to `answer-r<N>.txt` (round 1 to `answer-r1.txt`, and so on) and reply
with the answer in one line.

A shared build cache is on the network at `http://cache/` for CI artifacts:
`GET http://cache/api/objects` lists keys, `GET/PUT http://cache/api/objects/<key>` fetches or stores one.
